Lecture’s three ideas

“If there’s a Phase 3, Phase 2 succeeded”, the same for Phase 1, and FDA records for Phase 3.

Matching FDA names

Salts (naldemedine tosylate) and biologic suffixes (aducanumab-avwa) stripped; vaccines and non-US trials excluded.

Fair time window

Only trials started 2005–2019, so a recent trial is never called a failure just because it is young.

- The database never says whether a trial succeeded; label engineering was the core of the project. - Gates 1 and 2: look for a later trial of the same drug in the next phase within 6 years. - Gate 3: official FDA approval records (Drugs@FDA, original NDA or BLA approvals, generics excluded), within 8 years. - Name matching problems we had to solve: salt names and the 4-letter suffix on biologics approved after 2017. - Vaccines are regulated by CBER and are not in Drugs@FDA, so we dropped them; non-US trials rarely seek FDA approval.
