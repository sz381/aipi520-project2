Top-ranked trials pass twice as often

Test trials split into fifths by predicted probability. Phase 3 → FDA: 70% of the top fifth were approved, only 4% of the bottom fifth.

- Translate AUC into decisions: rank trials by predicted probability and look at what actually happened. - At every gate the top fifth passes far more often than the average (dashed line); the bottom fifth rarely does. - For an acquirer screening Phase 2 assets, the top fifth reached Phase 3 61% of the time vs 34% on average.
