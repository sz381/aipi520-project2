One drug, many names

MK-3475 = pembrolizumab = Keytruda. Synonyms learned from each trial’s “other name” field and expanded one hop only: a full transitive merge chained every chemotherapy into a single 32,000-name “drug”.

Background drugs ignored

“New drug X + cisplatin” must not look successful because cisplatin is in hundreds of later trials. A drug with 10+ earlier Phase 3 trials is treated as background.

- Instructor said the data is messy: free-text drug and disease names. This is where most of our effort went. - Parsing all XML takes about one minute; everything downstream reads one table. - Synonyms: one-hop expansion only; the naive approach produced one giant connected component. - Background drugs: without this rule, combination trials would be labelled successful by accident.
