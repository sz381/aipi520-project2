Models and evaluation

Learn from the past, test on the future

Train

trials started 2012 or earlier

Validate

2013–14

Test

2015 onward, scored once

Four models per gate

Logistic regression, random forest, gradient boosting, and a TF-IDF text model.

Chosen on validation

Hyper-parameters and the tree + text blend weight are picked on 2013–14 trials only.

Metrics

ROC-AUC (main), PR-AUC, Brier score, and 95% bootstrap confidence intervals.

- A temporal split mimics real use: we only ever know the past when we predict a new trial. - Four models per gate. The final model is the best tree model blended with the text model; everything is chosen on the validation years. - The test years are touched once, at the end. - Main metric ROC-AUC; we also report PR-AUC, calibration (Brier) and bootstrap confidence intervals.
