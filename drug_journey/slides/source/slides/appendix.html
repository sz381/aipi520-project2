18 framings we explored

Higher scores mixed easy cases: all sponsors (academic vs industry), mature drugs, healthy-volunteer trials.

Hardest: “primary endpoint met” from posted p-values, 0.63–0.64 within a phase. Design data says little about whether a drug works.

Our choice: the three-gate framing, with clean labels, new drugs only, and scores we can defend.

- Backup slide in case someone asks why we did not pick a framing with a higher AUC. - The same pipeline scored 0.63 to 0.85 depending on how success is defined and who is in the sample. - Higher numbers came from mixing populations that are trivially easy to separate.
