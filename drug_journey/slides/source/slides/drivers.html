Drug history drives every gate

19% → 60%

Phase 2 → 3 pass rate: 0 vs 3+ other trials of the drug in the last 2 years

13% → 58%

FDA approval rate: drug tested in 0–1 vs 6+ conditions before

27% vs 44%

Phase 1 → 2: crossover (PK-style) designs vs other Phase 1 trials

- Permutation importance: how much test AUC drops when one feature is shuffled. - Removing the whole drug-history group costs 0.11, 0.07 and 0.13 AUC at the three gates, more than any other group. - Plain-language version: a company that keeps running trials of a drug, in more diseases, is signalling conviction, and those drugs move on. - Surprise: a sponsor’s own past success rate adds nothing once drug history is known.
