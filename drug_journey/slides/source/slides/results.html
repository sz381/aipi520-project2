Results

All three gates are well above chance

Logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting land within 0.01–0.04 of each other: the limit is the information in public registry data, not the algorithm.

- Final test AUC: 0.718 for Phase 1 to 2, 0.722 for Phase 2 to 3, 0.807 for Phase 3 to FDA approval. Confidence intervals in the plot. - For reference, published Phase 2 models without proprietary data are around 0.6 to 0.7; InSilico reports 0.88 with molecular and omics data we do not have. - All three algorithms are close, so better data, not better models, is the lever.
